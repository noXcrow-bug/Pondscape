Pond builders serving all of Windsor‑Essex County

From the Detroit River shoreline to Lake Erie's north shore, we design, build, and maintain native ponds and bog filtration systems across the region.

Windsor

Windsor's mature, established neighbourhoods and the ravine lots along the Detroit River give us a lot to work with — from compact courtyard ponds to larger backyard installations with a full bog filtration cell.

LaSalle & Tecumseh

Newer subdivisions in LaSalle and Tecumseh often mean larger lots with more sun exposure, which suits a wider planting palette and larger bog cells for bigger ponds.

Amherstburg & Lakeshore

Properties closer to the Detroit River and Lake St. Clair shoreline already sit near naturally wet ground — we design drainage and basin placement around that, rather than fighting it.

Essex, Kingsville & Leamington

Essex County's agricultural clay soils hold water well, which is actually an advantage for pond installation once it's properly excavated and lined. Kingsville and Leamington's proximity to Point Pelee's marshland also means a deep local supply of naturalized reference points for planting design.

Don't see your town? We regularly work beyond this list throughout Essex County, including Belle River, McGregor, and Harrow. Call 226‑280‑3633 and we'll confirm coverage for your address.
